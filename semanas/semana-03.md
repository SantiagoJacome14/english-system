# Semana 3 · 2–8 noviembre
## El pasado: mi fin de semana y mis recuerdos

**🎯 Objetivo de la semana:** Contar qué hiciste el fin de semana pasado y un recuerdo de tu infancia.

## 📘 Gramática: Pasado simple (verbos regulares e irregulares)

- Regulares con **-ed**: *work → worked, study → studied, stop → stopped*. Pronunciación: /t/ *worked*, /d/ *played*, /ɪd/ *wanted*.
- Irregulares (¡apréndelos de memoria, 5 por día!): *go → went, have → had, see → saw, eat → ate, make → made, get → got, buy → bought, take → took, come → came, do → did*.
- Negativo y pregunta con **did** + verbo base: *I didn't go. Did you see it?* (nunca *did you went*).
- Expresiones: *yesterday, last night, last weekend, two days ago, when I was a kid*.

**Ejemplos (léelos en voz alta y cámbialos para que hablen de ti):**
- Last weekend I went to my aunt's house.
- I didn't do much on Sunday.
- Did you have a good weekend?
- When I was a kid, I lived in ___.
- Two years ago I started a new job.
