# Semana 8 · 7–13 diciembre
## Historias y anécdotas

**🎯 Objetivo de la semana:** Contar una anécdota de 3 minutos con principio, nudo y final, y reaccionar a las historias de otros.

## 📘 Gramática: Pasado continuo + pasado simple · conectores narrativos

- **Pasado continuo** (*was/were + -ing*) = acción en progreso en el pasado: *I was walking home…*
- Acción que interrumpe → pasado simple: *…when it started to rain.*
- **while** + continuo: *While I was cooking, the phone rang.*
- Conectores: *first, then, after that, suddenly, at that moment, in the end, finally*.

**Ejemplos (léelos en voz alta y cámbialos para que hablen de ti):**
- I was sleeping when you called.
- While we were driving, we saw an accident.
- Suddenly, the lights went off.
- In the end, everything was fine.
- What were you doing at 8 last night?
