# Semana 5 · 16–22 noviembre
## Comparar: ciudades, compras y comida

**🎯 Objetivo de la semana:** Comparar dos lugares, dos productos o dos comidas y dar tu preferencia con razones.

## 📘 Gramática: Comparativos y superlativos

- Cortos: *cheap → cheap**er** → the cheap**est***; *big → bigger → the biggest*.
- Largos: *expensive → **more** expensive → **the most** expensive*.
- Irregulares: *good → better → the best · bad → worse → the worst · far → farther → the farthest*.
- Igualdad: *as … as*: *It's **as** big **as** my old house.* · *not as … as*.

**Ejemplos (léelos en voz alta y cámbialos para que hablen de ti):**
- Bogotá is bigger than Medellín.
- This is the best restaurant in town.
- Online shopping is more convenient than going to stores.
- My new phone isn't as fast as I expected.
- What's the most beautiful place you've visited?
