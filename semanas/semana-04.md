# Semana 4 · 9–15 noviembre
## Planes y viajes

**🎯 Objetivo de la semana:** Explicar tus planes para diciembre y para 2027, y organizar un viaje.

## 📘 Gramática: Futuro: *going to*, *will* y presente continuo para planes

- **going to** = planes ya decididos: *I'm going to visit my parents in December.*
- **will** = decisiones en el momento, promesas, predicciones: *I'll call you later. It will be fun.*
- **Presente continuo** = citas fijas: *I'm meeting Ana on Friday.*
- Expresiones: *next week, this weekend, in two weeks, by the end of the year*.

**Ejemplos (léelos en voz alta y cámbialos para que hablen de ti):**
- I'm going to travel to ___ in December.
- I think it will rain tomorrow.
- I'm having dinner with my family on Saturday.
- What are you doing this weekend?
- I'll help you with that.
