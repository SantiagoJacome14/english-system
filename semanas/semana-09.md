# Semana 9 · 14–20 diciembre
## Sueños e hipótesis

**🎯 Objetivo de la semana:** Hablar de condiciones reales e imaginarias: *si pasa X…*, *si yo fuera…*

## 📘 Gramática: Condicionales 0, 1 y 2

- **Cero** (verdades): *If you heat water, it boils.*
- **Primero** (posible y real): *If it rains, I'll stay home.* (*if* + presente, *will* + verbo)
- **Segundo** (imaginario): *If I won the lottery, I would travel the world.* (*if* + pasado, *would* + verbo). Con *to be*: *If I **were** you…*
- *unless* = *if not*: *I won't go unless you come.*

**Ejemplos (léelos en voz alta y cámbialos para que hablen de ti):**
- If I have time, I'll call you.
- If I were rich, I'd buy a house by the beach.
- What would you do if you could live anywhere?
- If I spoke English fluently, I'd look for a job abroad.
- Unless we hurry, we'll be late.
