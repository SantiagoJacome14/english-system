# 🗝️ 50 frases esenciales para sobrevivir en una conversación

Apréndelas de memoria en la Semana 0–1. Son tu salvavidas.

## Cuando no entiendes
- Sorry, could you repeat that?
- Could you speak more slowly, please?
- What does "___" mean?
- How do you say "___" in English?
- Sorry, I didn't catch that.
- What do you mean?
- Can you give me an example?

## Para ganar tiempo (mientras piensas)
- Let me think…
- That's a good question.
- Well, …
- How can I put it…
- I mean…
- You know…
- Actually, …

## Para reaccionar
- Really?
- That's interesting!
- I see.
- That makes sense.
- No way!
- That's great! / That's too bad.
- Me too. / Me neither.
- I know what you mean.

## Para opinar
- I think… / I don't think…
- In my opinion, …
- I agree. / I don't agree.
- I'm not sure.
- It depends.
- To be honest, …

## Para mantener la conversación
- What about you?
- How about you?
- And you?
- Tell me more about…
- What do you think?
- Have you ever…?
- How was your day?

## Cortesía básica
- Nice to meet you.
- Excuse me…
- Thank you so much. / No problem. / You're welcome.
- Sorry for the delay.
- Can I ask you something?
- Could you help me, please?

## Para terminar
- It was nice talking to you.
- See you soon! / Talk to you later!
- Have a nice day!
- Take care!
