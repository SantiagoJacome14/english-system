# 📝 Test de nivel inicial (20 minutos)

No uses traductor ni diccionario. Si no sabes, escribe "no sé": eso también me ayuda. Copia tus respuestas en el hilo del proyecto y te digo tu nivel (A1, A2, B1, B2) y ajusto el plan.

## Parte 1 · Gramática y vocabulario (elige la opción correcta)

1. She ___ from Colombia. — a) am  b) is  c) are
2. I ___ coffee every morning. — a) drink  b) drinks  c) drinking
3. ___ you like music? — a) Are  b) Does  c) Do
4. There ___ two books on the table. — a) is  b) are  c) be
5. Yesterday I ___ to the supermarket. — a) go  b) went  c) gone
6. He is ___ than his brother. — a) tall  b) more tall  c) taller
7. I ___ TV when you called me. — a) watched  b) was watching  c) am watching
8. We ___ to Paris next summer. — a) are going to travel  b) travel  c) traveled
9. Have you ever ___ sushi? — a) eat  b) ate  c) eaten
10. I've lived here ___ 2020. — a) for  b) since  c) from
11. If it rains, I ___ at home. — a) stay  b) will stay  c) stayed
12. You ___ smoke here. It's forbidden. — a) mustn't  b) don't have to  c) shouldn't to
13. The book ___ by millions of people. — a) has read  b) has been read  c) is reading
14. If I ___ rich, I would buy a house by the sea. — a) am  b) were  c) will be
15. She asked me where ___. — a) did I live  b) I lived  c) do I live
16. I'm looking forward to ___ you. — a) see  b) seeing  c) saw
17. "Get along with" significa: — a) llevarse bien  b) levantarse  c) rendirse
18. "I'm broke" significa: — a) estoy roto  b) no tengo dinero  c) estoy cansada
19. If I had studied, I ___ the exam. — a) would pass  b) would have passed  c) passed
20. Despite ___ tired, she finished the project. — a) being  b) she was  c) to be

## Parte 2 · Comprensión lectora

Lee y responde en inglés:

> My name is Laura. I work in a small office in the city center. Every day I take the bus at 7:30, but last Monday the bus didn't come, so I walked to work. It took me forty minutes and I arrived late. My boss wasn't angry because it was the first time. Now I always check the bus app before I leave home.

1. Where does Laura work?
2. What happened last Monday?
3. Why wasn't her boss angry?
4. What does she do now?

## Parte 3 · Escritura (5–8 líneas)

Escribe en inglés sobre ti: quién eres, a qué te dedicas, qué te gusta hacer, y por qué quieres aprender inglés.
